# Smart Notes AI - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 22, 2025**

## Your Privacy is Our Priority

Smart Notes AI is built with privacy as the core principle. This policy explains how we handle your data (spoiler: we don't).

## Data Collection

**We collect ZERO data.**

- ❌ No account creation
- ❌ No cloud sync
- ❌ No analytics
- ❌ No tracking
- ❌ No third-party services
- ❌ No internet connection required

## Data Storage

All your data is stored **locally on your device only**:

- **Notes**: Stored in local SQLite database
- **Tasks**: Stored in local SQLite database
- **Reminders**: Stored in local notification system
- **AI Processing**: Happens entirely on-device

Your data never leaves your device. Period.

## AI Processing

- AI model runs **100% offline** on your device
- No data sent to external servers
- No cloud AI services used
- All processing is local

## Encryption

If you enable encryption:
- Notes are encrypted using AES-256
- Encryption keys stored in device secure storage (Keychain/Keystore)
- Only you can decrypt your notes

## Permissions

The app requests only essential permissions:

### Android
- **Notifications**: To remind you about tasks
- **Biometric**: For app lock (optional)
- **Microphone**: For voice notes (optional)

### iOS
- **Notifications**: To remind you about tasks
- **Face ID/Touch ID**: For app lock (optional)
- **Microphone**: For voice notes (optional)

**Note**: The app has NO internet permission.

## Third-Party Services

We use **zero** third-party services:
- No analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
- No crash reporting
- No advertising networks
- No social media SDKs

## Data Sharing

We don't share your data because we don't have access to it.

Your data is:
- ✅ On your device
- ✅ Under your control
- ✅ Private by default

## Data Deletion

To delete your data:
1. Uninstall the app
2. Or clear app data in device settings

Your data is permanently deleted.

## Export/Backup

If you choose to export your data:
- Export is encrypted
- You control the export file
- Export stays on your device until you share it

## Children's Privacy

Since we collect no data, there are no age restrictions. However, the app is designed for users 13+.

## Changes to This Policy

We'll update this policy if app functionality changes. Major changes will be announced in-app.

## Contact

Questions? Since we can't collect data or contact you, please review:
- App documentation
- GitHub repository (if open source)

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:
- ✅ Access: All data is in the app
- ✅ Modify: Edit anytime
- ✅ Delete: Uninstall or clear data
- ✅ Export: Use export feature

## Legal

This app:
- Complies with GDPR (we collect nothing)
- Complies with CCPA (we collect nothing)
- Complies with all privacy laws (we collect nothing!)

## Summary

**Smart Notes AI = Zero Data Collection**

Your notes. Your device. Your privacy.
